Our Story

Founded in 2010, Frontier Security Inc. emerged from a simple vision: to provide businesses and communities with security services that go beyond the ordinary. Our founders, veterans in law enforcement and security management, recognized the need for a company that combined traditional security expertise with modern technology.

Over the past 15 years, we've grown from a small local operation to a trusted name serving over 1,200 locations. Our success is built on unwavering commitment to our clients, continuous training of our personnel, and investment in cutting-edge security technology.

Today, Frontier Security Inc. stands as a leader in the industry, known for our professionalism, reliability, and innovative approach to security challenges.
